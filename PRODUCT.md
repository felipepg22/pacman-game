# Pac Mano

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop keyboard players seeking a short local arcade campaign, and portfolio visitors inspecting understandable game engineering.

## Product Purpose

A complete three-level maze-chase campaign. Collect every pellet, learn four ghost personalities, and finish in roughly 5–10 minutes.

## Brand Personality

Playful, geometric, restrained. An original retro arcade identity.

## Anti-references

Exact reproduction of existing arcade graphics or mazes, busy effects, online-service onboarding, and mobile-first controls are outside the confirmed design.

## Design Principles

- Put the playable maze at the center of the experience.
- Explain controls before the campaign begins and keep them visible.
- Make danger, frightened state, and harmless return distinguishable.
- Keep campaign status readable throughout play.
- Keep rules inspectable and independent of browser presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible HTML menus, visible focus, distinguishable ghost shapes independent of color, and reduced decorative motion respecting OS preference.
